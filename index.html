Quick Reference
ALTCS Medicaid
AZ resident 65+ or disabled. Income limit ~$2,829/mo. Asset limit ~$2,000. Covers AL, Memory Care, SNF. Not all facilities accept it.
VA Aid and Attendance
90+ days active duty with 1 wartime day. Single veteran up to $2,295/mo. Married up to $2,727/mo. Can combine with ALTCS in some cases.
Long-Term Care Insurance
Review policy for elimination period, daily benefit amount, and inflation protection. Most AL qualifies. Confirm with carrier before placement.
